Video to ASCII Art Converter

ZYRONMATRIX decodes a video in your browser, redraws each decoded frame as ASCII art, and can export the animation as a ZIP of numbered PNG files. It supports adjustable frame rate, resolution, colors, custom text, and keyframed grid density.

Why export a PNG image sequence?

A PNG sequence preserves every rendered frame without adding another round of video compression. Import the numbered files as an image sequence in After Effects, Premiere Pro, Blender, DaVinci Resolve, or another editor, then interpret the footage at the same FPS selected in ZYRONMATRIX.

Performance and memory

Sequence export stores rendered frames before ZIP packaging, so duration, resolution, and FPS directly affect memory use. For long or high-resolution videos, test a short clip first, lower the resolution or frame rate, and keep the tab active until the download begins.

Video privacy

The source video is decoded and rendered locally. ZYRONMATRIX does not upload video frames to its servers. A verified account is required for sequence export and cloud preset access, but the uploaded media remains local to the browser.

Video to ASCII FAQ

Why will my video not load?

Browser codec support varies. MP4 with H.264 video and WebM are commonly supported, but a file may need transcoding when its codec is unavailable in the current browser.

Does the ZIP contain a playable video?

No. It contains numbered PNG frames. Import them as an image sequence in video-editing software and assign the selected export frame rate.

Can grid density change during the video?

Yes. Add grid-size keyframes at different times; ZYRONMATRIX interpolates between them during playback and sequence rendering.
